# GLPConvert cold email positioning (2026)

## Primary positioning

GLPConvert is a white-label, clinic-owned transformation simulator and pre-consult conversion engine.

Core message:

- Most GLP-1 funnels collect lead details first.
- GLPConvert shows educational value first (simulation + economics), then captures higher-intent leads.

## Why clinics lease it

- Value before lead capture improves trust and consult intent.
- White-label ownership keeps clinic brand front and center.
- Transparent economics reduce uncertainty and improve follow-through.
- Provider-safe language guardrails reduce claims risk.
- Tenant-level analytics show simulator-to-lead conversion performance.

## One-liner options

- "Most GLP-1 funnels ask for contact details first. We show the estimated journey first."
- "Turn interest clicks into consult-ready leads with clinic-branded simulation and transparent economics."
- "Start with clarity, not guesswork: simulation first, lead capture second."

## Proof points to emphasize in demos

- 60-second low-friction simulation start.
- Timeline and milestone estimates before contact gate.
- Treatment economics snapshot with cautious language.
- Post-value lead capture and configurable conversion CTA.
- Structured metadata passed into lead pipeline for follow-up quality.

## Required compliance framing

- Educational estimate only.
- Results vary.
- A licensed provider determines final eligibility and treatment.
- No guaranteed outcomes or implied automatic approval.

## Objection handling

- "We already have a quiz." -> "This is not just a quiz; it is a value-first simulation + conversion layer that improves consult intent."
- "Will this conflict with medical rules?" -> "The flow is educational-only and provider-first by design."
- "Will this feel like our brand?" -> "It is deployed as white-label with clinic branding and configurable conversion routing."
