# GLPConvert wording library (v1)

Use this file as the canonical copy system for all intake, result, email, and sales surfaces.

## Non-negotiable disclaimers

- This tool is for educational and booking purposes only.
- Not medical advice.
- A licensed provider determines final eligibility and treatment decisions.

## Allowed recommendation patterns

- "Based on your answers, many patients explore programs like **[Program]**."
- "A provider will confirm whether this pathway is appropriate for you."
- "Pricing shown is an estimate and is confirmed by the clinic."

## Disallowed wording patterns

- "You qualify for [drug]"
- "You are approved"
- "Best dose for you is [X]"
- "Guaranteed weight loss"
- "Clinically proven for your case"

## CTA wording

- Primary: "Book your consult"
- Secondary: "See program options"
- Tertiary: "Talk to the care team"

Avoid: "Start treatment now", "Get your prescription instantly"

## Price wording rules

- Fixed: "Program price: $X" (only when contractually fixed)
- Starts-at: "Programs from $X"
- Range: "Typical range: $X-$Y"
- Always append: "Final pricing and eligibility are confirmed by the clinic."

## Consent wording

- Email consent: "I agree to receive email updates about my inquiry."
- SMS transactional consent: "I agree to receive booking-related text messages."
- Marketing opt-in (separate checkbox): "I agree to receive marketing messages."

## Footer legal line

- "Educational and booking purposes only. Not medical advice."
